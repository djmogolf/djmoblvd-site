A wedding DJ who also runs the room

Every package includes a professional emcee, so your grand entrance, toasts, first dance and send-off are announced on time. DJ Mo works with your planner and vendors behind the scenes so transitions are smooth.

What’s included

  • On-site ceremony music and wireless mics for your vows
  • Cocktail hour and reception music, with a customized personal playlist
  • Professional emcee for announcements and key moments
